# Idea Bank

The themes are proven by the reference page's top performers. The examples marked ★ were 10M+ view hits.

## Indoor glow-ups
- ★ Neon-outline heart wall reading corner (paint, corner shelves, LED, gallery wall, boucle chair)
- ★ Under-stair slatted wood wall with LED shelves and an indoor pebble plant garden
- Arched painted headboard with hidden LED and sconces
- Coffee-bar nook from an old dresser (open shelves, mug hooks)
- Bathroom towel cart from a pallet or crate, with baskets and plants
- Moon-circle painted wall plus a floating desk and fairy lights
- Closet-to-reading-nook (cushion bench, curtain, wall lamp)

## Outdoor and garden
- ★ Concrete planter or pillar with embedded glass-bottle "portholes"
- Glass-jar step lights cast into concrete stairs
- Tire, barrel or sewing-machine-base garden chair or bench
- Pallet vertical herb wall with drip irrigation
- Fire-pit circle with gravel, string lights and Adirondack chairs

## Objects and decor
- Textured black-and-gold geometric planter (paint split + brush reveal)
- Concrete candle holders and trays, poured and demoulded
- Resin river side table

## Seasonal (post 3–5 weeks ahead)
- Christmas porch planter (cut greens, berries, plaid bow, lantern)
- Valentine's heart wall or balloon arch
- Halloween porch, autumn harvest table, spring window boxes

## Concept test
A good concept passes all of these:
- the payoff is visible in one frame
- it uses a cheap or recycled material
- it has a cozy final shot
- it can be shown from one fixed angle
