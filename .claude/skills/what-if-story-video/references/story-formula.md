# Story formula (from "What If You Used 500 IQ to Save Your Sister? (Part 3)", Ifverse, 11:40)

## Voice rules

- **Second person, present tense.** "You're Leo. The smartest person alive. And at four twelve p.m., your phone rings."
- Short declaratives. Fragments. Ellipses for breath: "It never misses... ...and it never kills."
- Exact numbers everywhere — they make the genius feel real: *12 seconds, 5 clues, 40 floors, 50 guards,
  3:52, 18 accounts, 999 simulations, 28 floors in 8 seconds*.
- Rule of three and callbacks: a line planted early returns as the payoff ("Her eyes were mirrors…
  Tonight… so is the whole street", "I only need one", "Right behind you").
- Dialogue lines are short and quotable; the villain is calm and corporate, not cartoonish.
- Power is used **cleverly and non-lethally** — the hero out-thinks, never out-kills.

## Topic → premise recipe

1. Power: a single, specific ability (500 IQ, stop time for 10 s, read any lock, talk to animals…).
2. Cost: the power has made the hero hide / isolated (this is what the ending heals).
3. Stakes: someone they love, taken or endangered by people who *found them because of the power*.
4. Rule: a moral constraint (never kills / never lies / can't be seen) that forces clever solutions.
5. Emotional core: the person who noticed the hero all along → the ending's question.

## Series hooks

- Description + cold open recap the previous part in one line; end on an unresolved line if not final.
- Keep recurring numbers/motifs (the visor, the little green lights, "always one hundred").
