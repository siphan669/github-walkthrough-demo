## Description
Since you were seven, you've seen a number above every head: exactly how long each person has left. When you were nine, you tried to change your dad's. You couldn't. So you stopped looking.

Tonight, every timer outside Northfield Stadium reads the same time. 9:47 p.m. Forty thousand people. And your little brother is one of them.

The numbers always win. Unless they don't.

If you could see one person's number... would you look? Tell us in the comments.
