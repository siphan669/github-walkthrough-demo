## Description
You're a C student with headaches. Then lightning hits the hospital while you're inside the MRI — and you wake up with every neuron firing at once.

You read a 900-page book in four minutes. You learn Japanese before lunch. You save a stranger's life from across the room.

But a brain at 100% burns four times hotter. You have 72 hours. And the only machine that can turn it down belongs to a man who'd rather watch you burn.

Would you give up a genius brain to stay yourself? Tell us in the comments.
